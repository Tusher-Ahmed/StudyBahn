Step by step

How to apply to a German university

The usual path for international students, from shortlist to enrolment. Every university is a little different, so always follow the programme's own instructions.

Shortlist programmes (12–15 months before start)

Use the finder to filter by subject, language and deadline. Save 5–10 programmes and check each detail page for academic requirements: many Master's programmes ask for specific credits (ECTS) in certain subjects, a minimum grade, or a GRE.

Check your grade and degree recognition

Look up your degree and university in anabin (H+ means recognised). Convert your grade to the German scale with the modified Bavarian formula. Some countries need an APS certificate before applying (e.g. India, China, Vietnam), so check whether yours does.

Take your language test

Most English-taught programmes ask for IELTS Academic 6.0–7.0 or TOEFL iBT 80–95. Set your score in your profile and the finder will mark which programmes you qualify for. Some English-taught programmes still ask for basic German (A1/A2), sometimes only by graduation.

Prepare your documents

Each programme page has a checklist you can tick off: degree certificate, transcript, module descriptions, CV, motivation letter, letters of recommendation, language certificate and passport. Get certified copies early.

Apply: directly or via uni-assist

The programme page shows whether to apply through the university portal or through uni-assist. uni-assist checks your documents and takes 4–6 weeks, so submit at least 6 weeks before the deadline. Winter-semester deadlines are typically between 15 January and 15 July; summer-semester ones between July and 15 January.

Admission, blocked account and visa

After admission, open a blocked account (Sperrkonto) for the required yearly amount, get health insurance, and book your visa appointment immediately. In many countries the wait is several months. Export your deadline planner to your calendar so nothing slips.

Arrive and enrol

Pay the semester contribution, enrol (Immatrikulation), register your address (Anmeldung) within 14 days, and apply for your residence permit. Welcome to Germany! 🇩🇪

Rules and amounts (blocked account, APS, visa) change often. Check DAAD and the German mission in your country for current details.
